## How the sky works now
- The default is one fetch from the sky-view table.
- A half-res masked march (2 render-target attachments) is rendered only when the camera is above the air or within `1.5·clearAirDistance(h)` of an open edge.
- When it is on, each ray is gated by `skyGate`:
  - **Skip:** the ray lands inside the map at a steep angle (`dot(dir,up) < -0.2`), because the mesh covers it.
  - **Weight:** `smoothstep(0.003, 0.03, exp(-tau))`, with tau the Rayleigh optical depth to the outline.
  - The composite then picks table, masked pass or a mix.
- The camera must update before `tickBent`, because the masked pass reads the camera orientation.
