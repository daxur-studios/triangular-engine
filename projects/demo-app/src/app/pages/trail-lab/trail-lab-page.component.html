Ribbon trails & point-stamp decals

Track mode: drag across the ground to lay a strip via createTrailRibbonGeometry — a rover-tracks stand-in. Scorch mode: click to stamp a decal via createStampDecalGeometry (a thin wrapper around THREE.DecalGeometry) — an engine-scorch / leg-pad stand-in. Both are framework-free geometry builders; persistence, bucketing per terrain patch, and decay policy are app-side concerns (see docs/v3-planning/plans/ground-marks.md in brunos-space-program), not part of this engine utility.
