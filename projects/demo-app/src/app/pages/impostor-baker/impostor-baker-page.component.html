Octahedral impostor baker

createOctahedralImpostorAtlas bakes the procedural oak (left) from a grid of camera directions covering the upper hemisphere into the two textures previewed above the scene: albedo and packed normal+depth. buildOctahedralImpostorMesh then patches a real MeshStandardMaterial (still lit by the scene's lights) to pick and blend the 3 nearest baked views at render time — that's the single billboard to the right of the tree. The field stretching into the distance is a plain InstancedMesh sharing that exact geometry/material across thousands of copies, each orienting to the camera independently in the vertex shader.
