Deterministic ground scatter on plane, sphere, cylinder

Trees are placed by generateTerrainScatterInstances from deterministic candidate pools, then bucketed into near/far LOD tiers by bucketScatterInstancesByLod and rendered as one InstancedMesh per tier via buildScatterInstancedMeshesByLod. Grass has no far tier — it fades out by density via distanceFade instead of popping. Placement reads the same terrain surface sampler the ground mesh does, so instances never float or sink — and reject steep slopes. The view-distance slider scales every LOD/fade threshold directly (not the live orbit camera), with a hysteresis band on the tree tiers so scrubbing near a boundary doesn't flicker.
