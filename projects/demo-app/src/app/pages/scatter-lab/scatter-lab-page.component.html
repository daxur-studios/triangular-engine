Deterministic ground scatter on plane, sphere, cylinder

Trees are placed by generateTerrainScatterInstances from deterministic candidate pools, then bucketed into near/far LOD tiers by bucketScatterInstancesByLod and rendered as one InstancedMesh per tier via buildScatterInstancedMeshesByLod. Grass has no far tier — it fades out by density via distanceFade instead of popping. Placement reads the same terrain surface sampler the ground mesh does, so instances never float or sink — and reject steep slopes. The view-distance slider scales every LOD/fade threshold directly (not the live orbit camera), with a hysteresis band on the tree tiers so scrubbing near a boundary doesn't flicker. A lake basin is carved into the shared terrain field and rendered as a flat water patch at the same elevation; a suitability callback rejects any candidate at or below the waterline, so nothing spawns underwater. Trees additionally use a noise-based suitability mask to clump into forest patches instead of blanketing the terrain — grass stays uniform, so the patches read clearly against it. Grass and forest density are separate sliders so forest density can be pushed to full to see dense stands without also flooding the grass layer.
