Meadow: gusting wind over grass

{{ variantCount }} grass clump variants (buildGroundCoverClumpMesh, one seed each — no branching skeleton, unlike flora) are registered as a scatter species on a finer identity cell than trees typically use, so density reads as a field. Placement, streaming (selectFixedLevelScatterCells), and instancing (buildScatterInstancedMesh) are scatter's existing code, unchanged from the flora-scatter-lab pattern.

Every clump flutters on its own desynced timer (enableScatterWindSway, per-instance phase) — the same primitive flora-scatter-lab uses for trees. A gust (GRASS_WIND.gust) adds a second, coherent flow on top: curl noise (a smooth noise field's gradient, crossed with the local surface normal) sampled at world position and time, so nearby clumps bend together while the flow direction itself swirls and drifts instead of pointing one fixed way. The same noise field is also contrast-masked into patches with a visible boundary ("gust fronts"), so it reads as eddies drifting through the field rather than a uniform shimmer. Amplitude, eddy size, and drift speed are all live GPU uniforms (setGustAmplitude, setGustWavelengthM, setGustDriftSpeedMS).

Grass density fades out toward the view distance (generateTerrainScatterInstances's distanceFade) instead of rendering uniformly to the field's edge, and re-thins as the camera moves (ScatterStreamingService). A second, directional cull drops grass outside a conservative cone in front of the camera — a coarse angle-to-forward test, not a true 6-plane frustum, ported from brunos-space-program's cdlod-terrain-lab. Freeze it to detach the cull boundary from the live camera, then orbit/zoom freely to see exactly where it falls.
