3D view is M0 (site distribution and cell boundaries from buildPlanetGraphCore()) — the near-side cull only holds up for a far/orbit camera, see runbook 022. The 2D unwrap's plates/elevation/land modes are M1 (buildPlanetTectonics()). The temperature/moisture/biome/rivers modes are M2 (buildPlanetEcology()): latitude+elevation climate, Whittaker biome lookup, river tracing, and coastline extraction — rivers mode overlays traced river paths and closed coastline loops on the land/water base. "3D preview" (M3) is a flat-shaded triangle-fan mesh, one fan per cell, colored by whichever 2D mode is active — a debug view, not a finished renderer. Every vertex is now pushed out by cellCornerElevation() (M4a's O(1) lookup), clamped to sea level from its owning cell's land/water side so no ocean vertex renders above any land vertex; still no chunking, LOD, or crack-free stitching, that's M4b onward. Rivers mode also overlays the same traced river paths and coastline loops directly on the sphere, independent of the 3D preview toggle.
